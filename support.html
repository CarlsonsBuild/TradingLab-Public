I'm building TradingLab to explore trading ideas with repeatable tests and clear limits. Useful feedback, a little practical help, or voluntary support can all move the work forward.

You can inspect the public demonstration before deciding how to get involved. It uses synthetic inputs and a small educational validator. It does not establish trading results or private-system readiness.
